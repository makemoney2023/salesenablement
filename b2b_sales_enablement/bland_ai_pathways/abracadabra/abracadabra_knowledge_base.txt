# WHO ABRACADABRA IS

Abracadabra designs, builds, and runs AI-powered tools that fit the business, not the other way around.

Homepage line: "Custom software, built for how you work."
About line: "We build software around how you work."

Businesses whose work does not fit off-the-shelf software, or who are tired of juggling tools that each hold a piece of the customer.

# HOW A PROJECT WORKS

Stated on the homepage and repeated on the service pages.

Before anything is built:
- You review the plan.
- The right person decides.
- Speed does not skip review.
- Context is how you already work: customer, reviewer, owner, your terms. Missing info comes back.
- Your rules come first, so the whole build follows how you work.

One engineered plan, from request to finished outcome. Then five agents, named on the homepage:
1. Workflow Agent — rules, owners, handoffs. Rules mapped.
2. Experience Agent — screens, actions, states. Interface shaped.
3. Engineering Agent — data, logic, integrations. System assembled.
4. Quality Agent — main path and edge cases. Exceptions tested.
5. Launch Agent — demo and operating script. Demo prepared.

Service pages describe the same path in four beats:
- Plan. Map how the work gets done today, including the calls only a person should make, and agree on it before anything is built.
- Build. Built on Cloudflare and connected to the tools you already use, so nothing has to move.
- Review. Tested on your real work from last week before any customer sees it.
- Launch and run. Launch with close monitoring, keep it running, and you can change the rules at any time. It's yours to keep.

FAQ: "We learn how the work gets done today, build the software around it, and launch it with you. You keep the system, plus a short film and script to show it off."
About: every system shipped comes with a short film of the real screens. Work is built on Cloudflare, tested on real work, and kept running after launch.

Homepage on scale: apps built with Cloudflare grow with the business, from the first user to millions, without a rebuild, and stay fast, secure, and available wherever customers are.

Requirements named on the homepage:
- Finished result delivered
- Right person decides
- Speed without skipping review

## Search and AI visibility
https://abra-ca-dabra.app/services/ai-visibility/
"Show up when someone asks."
"Get found in AI search."
More customers ask AI tools before they ever visit a website. The work is to make those answers point at the business, and to show who is reading the site.
You cannot fix a bad answer by arguing with the model. You shape it by what you publish, which pages you offer to be read, and which crawlers you refuse.
What you get, in the site's words:
- A note in your words. A plain file tells a reader, human or crawler, what you do and which pages are the ones to cite. It does not invent a claim.
- You choose who reads your site. Let search tools in, keep data-harvesting bots out.
- A page worth quoting. The public page answers a real question in language you will stand behind.
- A record of who came. You can see the crawlers that fetched the site.
The site is explicit: an SEO retainer, a hire, and a visibility system all fail at the same thing. None of them can force a model to mention you. Abracadabra will not pretend otherwise. Do not guarantee a mention.

## Customer service
https://abra-ca-dabra.app/services/voice-and-realtime-agents/
"Answer callers from your records, with a person when needed."
"AI that answers your phone."
The line says what the record says. It does not fill a silence with a plausible sentence. When it does not know, it says so and hands the call over. Calls go to staff with a short summary so nobody repeats themselves. It takes bookings and messages, and only does what it has been allowed to do. You can replay the call and the note.
After close, the line stays up, answers the closed-hours list, and takes the rest as a message with the call attached.
The site says when not to buy this: a few calls a day that are already handled well, or calls that need judgment all the way through. Keep the person on a quiet line.

# READINESS CHECK

https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/
"Is your business ready to put AI to work?"
A five-to-seven-minute check. One question per screen. You can leave and come back with the same link.
What it measures:
- Pressure. The jobs that eat the week, and what they cost. (The page does not publish a dollar formula. Do not invent one.)
- Readiness. Whether the data, the workflow, the owner, and the decision can support real work.
- Growth. Where new customers come from, and whether that path is something you control.
- Visibility. A live read of how answer engines can find and quote the public site. The last scored question asks for a public website. If that read fails, the rest of the report still comes back.
What you get: a band, four scores, three suggestions in each section, and a thirty-minute working session if you want one.
After the check: you leave an email, then see the scores and a way to book that session.
It is a working picture of the business as described, plus a public read of the site. It is not medical, legal, or financial advice. It is not a diagnosis.

The homepage FAQ matches this: five to seven minutes, then a thirty-minute working session if you want one.
